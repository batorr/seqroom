    About seqroom

    seqroom is an experimental collaborative step‑sequencer built with Node.js, Express, and a modular browser‑based audio engine. Its goal is to explore real‑time, low‑latency music creation directly in the browser.

    With thanks to Gergely Vincze for his many valuable ideas and feedback during development, and Péter Hermann for his help refactoring the project.🩶
